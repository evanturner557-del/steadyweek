Privacy notice

Last updated 19 October 2026

Who we are

Operated by a UK sole trader trading as Steadyweek. Contact: steadyweek@agentmail.to. Postal address available on written request. Steadyweek is a Google Sheets workspace for UK small businesses. For anything in this notice, email steadyweek@agentmail.to. We are the “controller” (the organisation responsible) for the personal data described here.

What we collect

  • If you email us: your name, business name, email address and your message.
  • If you buy: your name, email and order details. Payments and downloads are handled by our payment provider, which tells us your name, email, what you bought and whether payment succeeded. We never see your full card number.
  • Your copy of the template stays on your own computer or in your own Google Drive. We cannot see it unless you share it with us.
  • Setup calls: if you share your Sheet with us for a setup call, we see what is in it (which may include details of your own customers and leads). We use it only to set up your Sheet, on your instructions, and we don’t keep a copy. Please remove our access when the call is finished. For this, we act on your behalf as your “processor”.
  • Any calculator or checklist on this site runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to us.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies. We count page visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service (goatcounter.com). It records the page, the referring site, browser screen size and an approximate country, but sets no cookies, does not store your IP address and does not track you across sites. Visits from browsers that send Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control are not counted. We use these counts only to see which pages are useful (legitimate interests) and keep them for no longer than 24 months. Fonts are served from our own site. Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Why we use your data (lawful basis)

  • Replying to enquiries: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.
  • Delivering what you bought: performance of our contract with you.
  • Taking payment and preventing fraud: contract, and our and our payment provider’s legitimate interests.
  • Keeping business and tax records: legal obligation.

We never sell your data.

Who we share it with

  • AgentMail, which hosts our email inbox;
  • our payment provider, for payments and downloads (named here before payments open);
  • Google, if you share your Google Sheet with us for a setup call;
  • our website host (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc.).

AgentMail, Google and GitHub are based in the United States, so personal data we share with them may be stored or processed there. Google relies on the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (the ‘UK–US data bridge’) for transfers from the UK (see Google’s privacy frameworks page). GitHub handles our website host’s visitor logs under its own privacy statement. GitHub relies on the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (the ‘UK–US data bridge’) for transfers from the UK (see GitHub’s privacy statement). We do not have a separate data processing agreement with AgentMail, Inc., which hosts our email inbox in the United States, so emails sent to us are handled under AgentMail’s privacy notice. That notice says AgentMail may use the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for international transfers, and that, without such an agreement, it may use email content to improve and train its own services. Please don’t send us sensitive personal information by email. You can ask us for details at any time.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to an order: deleted 30 days after our last contact. Order details, including anything you share with us for a setup call or check-in and our working copies, are deleted 30 days after we deliver. We keep only a basic order record (your name, email, date, what you bought and the amount) for as long as tax law requires (at least 5 years after the 31 January tax-return deadline for the year concerned).

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. Email us to ask. Requests sent to our inbox are handled by the owner (not by automatic replies) within one month, as the law requires.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please tell us first by emailing steadyweek@agentmail.to with “Data complaint” in the subject line. The owner will acknowledge it within 30 days, look into it without undue delay, keep you updated and tell you the outcome. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
