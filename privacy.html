Privacy notice

Last updated 6 October 2026

Who we are

Steadyweek is a trading name of a sole trader based in the UK. The owner’s full legal name and UK business address will be added here before we take any payment. Steadyweek is a Google Sheets workspace for UK small businesses. For anything in this notice, email steadyweek@agentmail.to. We are the “controller” (the organisation responsible) for the personal data described here.

What we collect

  • If you email us: your name, business name, email address and your message.
  • If you buy: your name, email and order details. Payments and downloads are handled by our payment provider (we will name it here before we take any payment), which tells us your name, email, what you bought and whether payment succeeded. We never see your full card number.
  • Your copy of the template stays on your own computer or in your own Google Drive. We cannot see it unless you share it with us.
  • Setup calls: if you share your Sheet with us for a setup call, we see what is in it (which may include details of your own customers and leads). We use it only to set up your Sheet, on your instructions, and we don’t keep a copy. Please remove our access when the call is finished. For this, we act on your behalf as your “processor”.
  • Any calculator or checklist on this site runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to us.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party trackers. Fonts are served from our own site. Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Why we use your data (lawful basis)

  • Replying to enquiries: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us.
  • Delivering what you bought: performance of our contract with you.
  • Taking payment and preventing fraud: contract, and our and our payment provider’s legitimate interests.
  • Keeping business and tax records: legal obligation.

We never sell your data.

Who we share it with

  • AgentMail, which hosts our email inbox;
  • our payment provider, for payments and downloads (named here before payments open);
  • Google, if you share your Google Sheet with us for a setup call;
  • our website host (GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc.).

Some of these providers are based in, or use servers in, the United States. We are confirming the contract terms and the legal basis each provider uses to protect personal data sent outside the UK. This section will name them once confirmed. You can ask us for details at any time.

How long we keep it

Enquiries: up to 12 months after our last contact. Customer records: for as long as tax law requires (at least 5 years after the 31 January tax-return deadline for the year concerned).

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive it in a portable format. Email us and we will respond within one month.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please tell us first by emailing steadyweek@agentmail.to with “Data complaint” in the subject line. We will acknowledge it within 30 days, look into it without undue delay, keep you updated and tell you the outcome. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.
