FAQ

Common questions

Do I need any paid software?

No. Steadyweek is built for Google Sheets, which is free with a Google account. The file is a standard .xlsx, but we haven’t tested it in Microsoft Excel yet, so we don’t promise that it works there.

Can you see my data?

No. You keep the template on your own computer or in your own Google Drive. We never have access unless you share it with us for a setup call.

Is this accounting software?

No. It's a planning and weekly-review tool. Keep your bookkeeping in your accounting software and your accountant in the loop.

What if I get stuck?

The QuickStart guide covers the basics, and the setup tier includes a live call. If a file doesn’t work as described, you’re entitled to have it fixed or replaced, or to a refund, as set out in our terms.

Can my team use it?

Yes. Share the Sheet with whoever needs it, using Google's normal sharing settings.

When can I buy it?

We’re finishing the first version. Ordering opens at launch, and this page will be updated as soon as it does. No payment is taken today.
